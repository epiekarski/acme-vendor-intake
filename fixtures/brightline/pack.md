# Research pack: Brightline Analytics, Inc.

<!-- Completed example used by `make demo`. In the live demo the Bot drafts this itself. -->

## Company overview

- Product analytics for B2B SaaS, focused on account-level feature adoption and renewal ([home](https://brightline-analytics.example/))
- Founded 2019, headquartered in Austin, Texas, about 120 employees ([home](https://brightline-analytics.example/))

## Intended use

90-day RevOps pilot: sync Acme product usage events, tied to customer accounts, to see which features drive renewals. Requested by Priya Shah.

## Security and trust

- SOC 2 Type II report available to customers under NDA ([trust](https://brightline-analytics.example/trust))
- Standard DPA published ([DPA](https://brightline-analytics.example/legal/dpa))
- AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit ([trust](https://brightline-analytics.example/trust))
- Customer data deleted within 30 days of termination ([trust](https://brightline-analytics.example/trust))
- Breach notice is "without undue delay", with no stated time limit ([trust](https://brightline-analytics.example/trust))

## Pricing

- Growth plan at $1,200 per month fits the pilot; Starter is free up to 10,000 events ([pricing](https://brightline-analytics.example/pricing))
